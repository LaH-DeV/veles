14. (2026-09-17) The built-in method list moved to Veles: `extend` blocks in std/prelude/string.vs
   and list.vs provide trim/split/lines/replace/indexOf/repeat/toUpper/toLower/toF64/padStart/padEnd,
   take/drop/slice/count/zip/flatMap/distinct/chunked/windowed/sortedDescending/min/max/sum,
   insert/removeAt/addAll/sort, Range step/contains/reversed/len, plus `panic(msg)` and `xs.at(-1)`.
   Only primitives stay in Go (sema/builtins_doc.go is now that list): string byteAt/bytes,
   List<u8>.decodeUtf8, and the pre-existing ones. Still open from the old list:
   - Channel: trySend(x): bool, tryRecv(): T? (non-suspending) — runtime work, not prelude
   - int: saturatingMul (llvm.smul.fix.sat with scale 0)
   - not as built-ins, on purpose: toString() (interpolation), parse<T>() (wants a FromString
     trait once traits on primitives are allowed), width/precision formatting (a format module)
   - the Go-lowered eager adapters (sema/lower_list.go, check_map.go) could move to the prelude
     too once one is benchmarked at -O2 against the Veles version
   - `min()`/`max()`/`sorted()` need numbers or strings; with a generic `extend<T> List<T>` the
     error for other T is reported inside the prelude. An `Ord` marker bound would fix the location.
